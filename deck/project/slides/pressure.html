Act 3
Act three asks where the pressure lands. The Urban Displacement Project’s typology is the standard map of displacement risk. A simple logistic model on year-2000 census data predicted which tracts went on to lose low-income households far better than its vulnerability flag: 0.78 against a coin-flip 0.52. After 2018 the flag actually points the wrong way for price and rent growth, because pressure moved into wealthier tracts. Meanwhile, in the low-income tract types, asking rents already eat more than half of income.

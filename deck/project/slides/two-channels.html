And no measurable bust

Layoff notices per worker: no trend over 13 quarters (p = 0.66).

Entry-level postings are not scarcer in AI-exposed jobs: 24% of their postings vs 4% in AI roles.

Workers’ AI-displacement talk on Reddit is falling, about 1.3% lower odds each month.

An unequal boom, not a visible bust.

Act 1
Who captures the gains? Two different groups. White households hold 70 percent of capital income but are under-represented in AI jobs. Asian workers are the mirror image: twice their share in AI jobs, but the most dependent on wages. Black and Hispanic workers sit at about a quarter of parity in both channels. And the “AI is destroying jobs” story doesn’t show up: layoffs are flat, entry-level postings aren’t scarcer, and worker anxiety online is falling. So this is an unequally shared boom, not a bust.

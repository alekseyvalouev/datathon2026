Act 4
Act four follows the households that leave. Compared with similar people who stayed, Central Valley leavers are 16 points more likely to pay half their income in rent, 9 points more likely to commute an hour or more, and earn 26 thousand dollars less. Homeownership is the one thing that improves. Keeping the Bay Area job and commuting back usually doesn’t work either: at full car cost, it leaves a negative budget in 35 of 40 job-and-city combinations. These are comparisons measured after the move, not causal estimates.

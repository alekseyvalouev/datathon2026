Model: data decisions
Data decisions. From 23 thousand same-unit turnovers we keep re-listings from mid-2022, when we have state income limits, and units a low-income household could still afford before the turnover: 12 thousand units, of which 19 percent left reach. We split by time: train on earlier re-listings, tune on late 2025, and score 2026 exactly once. We hand-checked 30 random units against the raw listing history. All 30 reproduced, and reading them made us change the label: some first asking rents crossed the line and were quickly cut back, so we use the settled asking rent.

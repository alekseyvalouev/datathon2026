The data
We used four of the datathon datasets — Zillow, Indeed, Reddit and Glassdoor, and Google Maps — and joined them with public sources: IRS county migration, Census microdata and commute flows, California layoff notices and state income limits, the Urban Displacement Project’s tract typology, and twenty-eight years of San Francisco Planning Commission minutes. Each workstream has code that rebuilds its results from the raw files.

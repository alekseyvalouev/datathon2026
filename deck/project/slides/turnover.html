Act 2
Act two follows the home rather than the neighborhood. We matched the same unit across consecutive listings: 23 thousand pairs. California caps rent increases for sitting tenants but not for a vacant unit, and in 2026 almost a quarter of turnovers jumped faster than that cap. In San Francisco, one in five turnovers took a unit out of reach for a household at 80 percent of area median income, while almost none came back. And employers are recruiting the replacements: relocation help shows up on 8 percent of high-wage postings and almost never on low-wage ones.

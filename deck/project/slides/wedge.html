Jobs to housing
This is the hinge between jobs and housing. In no Bay Area city does the median clerical, support or frontline salary cover the median rent at 30 percent of income. For AI and machine-learning roles it does in 16 of 23 cities. The jobs story doesn’t move people by firing them; it moves them by setting the price of the housing around them.

Act 5
Act five is why this stays invisible. Over 2011 to 2021, migration actually lowered the region’s average income rise by 8 percent: lower-income leavers and younger arriving households cancel out. So county averages hide both stories. And the usual villain, public hearings, isn’t where housing is stopped: in San Francisco 90 percent of contested projects were approved and the Commission followed staff 97 percent of the time. Opposition buys delay and division, not denial.

Act 1
The prize is real. AI-positioned work pays a median of 178 thousand dollars against 81 thousand in clerical, support and junior professional work, and these occupations hire about 3.8 times more per current worker. The bars compare what new postings offer with what people already in the job earn. AI-related classes advertise up to 1.5 times incumbent pay; exposed classes advertise below it. Because postings are 2026 dollars and incumbents are 2023 dollars, the below-one ratios are even worse in real terms.

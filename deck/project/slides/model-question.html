The model
Here’s the model. Everything so far says the moment that matters is a turnover: rent control protects the sitting tenant, not the next one. So we ask, for a unit a low-income household could still afford, when it’s re-listed, will the next rent push it past the line? The line is the state’s 80 percent of area median income limit with the standard three-times-rent rule. Features come from all three analysis workstreams: the unit and its market, its neighborhood, and the job mix of its city.

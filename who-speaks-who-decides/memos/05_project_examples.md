# Memo 5: representative projects

Read from the hearing record. Addresses are from public agendas. Comment counts are support/oppose marks across all of a project's hearings.

## Routine approvals
- **31A Coleridge Street (2023).** Legalizing a rebuilt 450-square-foot rear cottage. One supporter, no opponents. Approved 6-0 in one hearing as staff recommended.
- **1721 15th Street (2022).** 50 homes in two buildings under the state density bonus. Five opposed, seven in support. Approved 6-1 in one hearing.

## Modified through discretionary review
- **2783 Diamond Street (2017).** New four-story house on a vacant lot; six opponents. Staff advised against review. The Commission took it and required the top floor removed, the façade redesigned and a deck reduced.
- **701 Hampshire Street (2018).** Two accessory units plus a fourth-floor addition to a six-unit building. After four hearings, approved with the fourth floor eliminated.
- **144 Peralta Avenue (2019).** Rear addition to a house; two opponents. Review declined, approved as proposed 6-0.

## Delayed, then approved
- **650 Divisadero Street.** 66 homes, six stories. Twelve hearing dates from July 2016 to November 2018; 45 opposing and 34 supporting comments. Final vote 6-0 with an added tree-protection condition.
- **429 Beale Street.** 144 homes. Three hearings in spring 2018; motions to approve failed twice. Passed after a 45-foot-wide notch was cut into the top four floors.
- **2918 Mission Street.** 75 homes in eight stories. In September 2018 the Commission adopted a motion of intent to disapprove, 4-3; two weeks later it approved, 4-3.
- **1525 Pine Street (2021).** 21 homes under the density bonus. Continued once "with direction to explore a project that provides more light"; approved 4-2 with lighting conditions.

## Disapproved
- **668-678 Page Street (2018).** Converting a six-unit rental building to condominiums. 22 opposed, 6 in support. Denied 7-0 against staff's original recommendation.
- **2406 Bush Street (2019).** Legalizing the merger of two flats into one home. Staff recommended denial; 5-0.
- **1600 Jackson Street (2018).** Chain grocery store with eight homes above. 126 opposing and 119 supporting comments over six hearings. Denied 4-0.
- **1196 Columbus Avenue (2017).** 75-room hotel. Denied 6-0 against staff's recommendation.
- **116 10th Avenue (2017).** House addition under discretionary review; 13 opposed, none in support. Denied 6-0, rare for that process.

None of these five is new multi-unit housing. This rests on the examples read, not a tally of all disapprovals.

## Stalled or unresolved
- **471 24th Avenue (2016-17).** Effective demolition of a single-family house. Continued five times, once "with direction from the Commission", then continued indefinitely.
- **9 Apollo Street (2020).** Removing an unauthorized unit by merging it into the home above. Continued with direction, then indefinitely.
- **A batch of seven student-housing conversion cases (2016-17).** Requests to legalize conversion of residential hotel rooms and homes to student housing, continued together four times and then indefinitely. Counted as one matter after deduplication.
- **A six-unit condominium conversion in North Beach (2020).** A motion to approve failed 3-3; no further action recorded.
- **1010 Mission Street (2022).** A motion to continue failed 2-3 and a motion to disapprove failed 3-2; coded as a deadlock.

## Misclassifications these examples exposed
- 137 Clayton Street and 2918 Mission Street: motion of intent to disapprove, later approved.
- 60 Clifford Terrace: the Zoning Administrator's "intent to deny" a variance was read as a Commission disapproval; the Commission approved.
- 88 Broadway: shown as undecided although the record says the environmental appeal was rejected.
- 1151 Washington Street: a review request withdrawn before hearing, which means the project proceeds.
